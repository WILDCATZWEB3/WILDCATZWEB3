# Hey, I'm Catz 🐾

I build in web3, mostly around Solana and Robinhood Chain: Telegram bots, onchain tooling, and community products. Based remote, building in public.

## What I'm working on

**CATZ** is a project that helps people find early web3 projects and memecoin opportunities with clearer risk context.

- 🤖 **[@Catzintellectbot](https://t.me/Catzintellectbot)**: a Telegram bot that posts Solana and Robinhood Chain memecoin calls and early project drops, with PnL cards and a history of past projects.
- 🌐 **[CATZ portal](https://catz-bot-production.up.railway.app/portal)**: sign in with Telegram and see how it works.
- 📣 **[Catzcook on Telegram](https://t.me/Catzcook)**: community channel.

> Calls are information, not financial advice. Memecoins are high risk and most go to zero. Always do your own research.
